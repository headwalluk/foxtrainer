# Experience levels

> **Draft.** The exact prefs behind each level are being curated. This page will list them,
> generated from the catalogue.

## Overall feel

| Level | Intent |
|---|---|
| **Lean** | Strip Firefox back: no sponsored content, fewer prompts and extras, a quieter browser |
| **Balanced** | Sensible defaults for most people: clutter removed, everything useful kept |
| **Full-fat** | Keep the bells and whistles; just remove tracking, telemetry and sponsored content |

## AI features

| Choice | Intent |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Block every AI feature, local and cloud |
| **Local only** | Allow features that run on your device (such as translations); block cloud AI |
| **Everything** | Leave Firefox's AI features available |

## Privacy

| Level | Intent |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Firefox's protections plus telemetry and studies off |
| **Strict** | Stronger tracking protection and cleaner defaults; very little breakage |
| **Hardened** | Maximum protection, drawing on arkenfox. **Some sites will break** |

## Other questions

- **HTTPS-only:** refuse insecure connections unless you allow a site.
- **Language and spelling:** interface language, spellcheck dictionaries and the languages sent to websites.
